How it Works

Precision control in three layers.

01

Sense

The UPQC continuously monitors voltage, current, harmonics, and power factor at microsecond resolution — building a complete real-time picture of grid conditions.

02

Compute

Onboard processing calculates optimal compensation signals using advanced control algorithms, reacting to disturbances before they propagate downstream.

03

Compensate

Solid-state power electronics inject precise correction signals in under 1ms — eliminating voltage sags, swells, harmonics, and reactive power in real time.

New — no added hardware

GPU burst-mode compensation

Synchronous training alternates a compute-heavy phase near GPU TDP with a near-idle all-reduce or checkpoint phase, every few seconds, across every GPU in the job. At scale that becomes a single megawatt waveform concentrated in the 0.2–3 Hz band — inside the resonant range of transmission lines and turbine-generator shaft torsionals. The UPQC's existing converter stage is re-tasked to cancel it on the wire, with no additional battery, capacitor, or filter hardware.

The same power electronics that regulate voltage and filter harmonics are driven, simultaneously, as a bidirectional buffer against the GPU compute/communication cycle — charging on the idle phase, discharging on the compute phase.

  • Sense-to-compensate loop< 50 ms
  • Critical frequency band handled0.2 – 3 Hz
  • Additional hardware requiredNone
  • Peak swing, unmitigated± 41%
  • Peak swing, compensated± 6%
  • Spectral energy reduction, critical band~90%
